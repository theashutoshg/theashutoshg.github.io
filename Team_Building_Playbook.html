Best Practices

Hire Slow, Fire Fast

Take time to find the right fit. Don't compromise on culture or skills. Act quickly when it's not working.

Document Everything

If it's not written, it doesn't exist. Create processes, playbooks, and runbooks for everything.

Small Autonomous Teams

Keep teams small (4-6 engineers). Give them ownership and autonomy to make decisions.

Async-First Communication

Don't expect immediate responses. Document decisions. Respect time zones. Over-communicate context.
